# Acme Corp Leave Policy (2026)

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Parental Leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance.

## How to Apply
All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal. Annual leave of more than 5 consecutive days must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance. Questions should be directed to hr@acme.example.
